#+title: campaign-idea

Theme: (in)significance

First they will scale a mountain, (falling rocks and similar hazards, there will be rumbling and earthquakes, rayscha or whatever will attack and their goal is to get past them to higher altitude.)
The sesson concludes with them finding someone - an old servant from legend - who looks them in the eyes and informs them tha the gods have fled.


Use their gods of hades for session 2 - to do with beneath

Session 2 starts with the entire spine of the mountain range exploding.



One player will have a story from their youth, in which by tradition a carcass is left for 'lesser creatures' to feast on.
"As the gods grant us, so we now grant them. We are nothing, compared to them."
