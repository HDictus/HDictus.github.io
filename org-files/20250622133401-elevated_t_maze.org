#+title: elevated-T-maze

An experimental setup used in expeirments of fearful rat behaviors.

The experiments take place on an elevated T-shaped platform.
The bottomost arm of the T is sheltered on all sides.
You can place rats in the enclosed space and measure their tendency to stay put,
or put them in the open space and measure how fast they scurry to safety.
