#+title: murrough-serotonin-2011

https://www.biologicalpsychiatryjournal.com/article/S0006-3223(11)00076-X/abstract


Murrough and Charney discuss gene-environment interactions in the [[id:13fd201a-d462-4e74-a96e-643c48e0b803][5HTTLRP]] gene with respect to anxiety.
For example, they mention a study in which there was an association between maternal anxiety during gestation and infant negative emotionality only when a long-allele version was present.
