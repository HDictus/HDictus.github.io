#+title: sheikh-examination-2008

Sheikh et al. examined relationships between [[id:13fd201a-d462-4e74-a96e-643c48e0b803][5HTTLRP]] polymorphisms and attributional style.
They found increased depressogenic attributions in people with the short allele.
