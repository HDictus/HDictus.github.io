#+title: carli-role-2020
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-psychiatry/article/abs/protective-genetic-variant-for-adverse-environments-the-role-of-childhood-traumas-and-serotonin-transporter-gene-on-resilience-and-depressive-severity-in-a-highrisk-population/F07C8A85043155818BA8A43D540358AF https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0191886908001979?casa_token=KEiuiROetgkAAAAA:iG2hqpJ9uQ5gubCuVlTRK0dI3O54yWqdQZdHgsDxUkLwcw32-5-gmA60E7HO8D5mq5DqaB0FDA

Another study on the relationship of [[id:13fd201a-d462-4e74-a96e-643c48e0b803][5HTTLRP]] polymorphism to depression and resilience.
They saw no direct relationship to resilience or depression, but an interaction between the gene and childhood trauma.
Interestingly, they found that the long allele was assocated with lower resilience after severe childhood trauma.
This conflicts with [[id:a996c379-6371-4981-8fc1-e74e8e9c263a][murrough-serotonin-2011]], in which the short allele was the one associated with worse resilience.
