#+title: wonderbook-writing-challenge-1

The painter's eye on the batfish, shaping the divine, spinning hideousness into wonder.
The explorer's eye delights in the strange, revulsion is a source of mirth.
But seriously the painter looks, aiming to capture every detail which makes the scene so strange.

The batfish ascends, swimming, and jumps from the water.
The painter blinks, he has his scene. But it is locked to his memory, he cannot refer back to it.

Later, in his studio, he will recreate the scene from memory with the materials he has on hand.
He will sculpt in order to capture every relevant trick of lighting, and then he will augment it with his memory.
The batfish he will paint is not the one he sees now, but only a related creature.
The offspring of that fish, and his atelier, gestated in the egg of his mind.
But he will regard his work, and be satisfied.
